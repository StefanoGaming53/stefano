See Through Static: The Collection is an anthology of psychological and eldritch horror experiences that follow different people as they become entangled in a series of disturbing events. From the paranoia-inducing recordings of See Through Static, to the unsettling investigations of Sounds From Silence, the desperate broadcasts of Speak With Screams, and the grim discoveries of The Morgue, each story uncovers another piece of a much larger mystery. As fear spreads and reality begins to unravel, strange deaths and inexplicable phenomena hint at something lurking beneath the surface. Something ancient. Something hungry. And something that may have already set it's sights on you.

See Through Static:
See Through Static is a 2d first-person point and click psychological horror that features one of if not the most overbearing atmosphere in the collection. Perfectly reflecting that dreadful feeling of being alone in a dark room late at night with only a screen and the dim moon as your only light. Seeing shadows out of the corner of your eye and wondering if something was really there or if your mind was just playing tricks on you. In your paranoid state you must find hidden clues within 5 cryptic VHS tapes. The question remains, are you making connections to some big conspiracy or getting lost in your paranoia.

Sounds From Silence:
Sounds From Silence is a 2d first-person point and click psychological horror where you play as William Kane, the lead detective investigating the disturbing recent suicide cases. Initially believed to be caused by an abnormal wave of mass hysteria, you quickly realize something else may be at fault. Search through different radio signals to find clues and evidence to help close this case before it spreads out of control. Will you discover the truth before your obsession consumes you?

Speak With Screams:
Speak With Screams is a 2d first-person point and click psychological horror where you play as Jeffery Moore, a news anchor who reports on the recent cases of mass hysteria. Use your pc to search up information and write your reports. However, when the day comes that there's no stories.... what would you do for views?

The Morgue:
The Morgue is a 3d first-person psychological horror where you play as Sarah Morrow, an apprentice mortician working under her father at the family Morgue. Examine and write reports on bodies, investigate the recent deaths, and experience vivid dreams that twist the line between nightmares and reality. Will you be there to expose the truth... or die trying?
